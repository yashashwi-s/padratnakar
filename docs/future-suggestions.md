# Later: reader suggestions

Deferred feature, not implemented or published. Provide a simple anonymous form for suggestions and text corrections with optional contact information. Automatically attach the relevant pad/collection and app version when available. Let the sender preview what is submitted. Collect only useful, disclosed metadata; avoid device fingerprinting.

An owner-only portal should list submissions, their context, status and replies. A private receipt link can let anonymous senders return to read replies without an account; optional email replies would require their consent and delivery setup. Keep submitted corrections separate from canonical text until reviewed. Include basic spam controls, length limits and a retention policy before launch.
