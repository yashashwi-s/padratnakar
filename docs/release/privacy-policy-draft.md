## Information handling

Based on the current application design, Pad Ratnakar does not require an account, sign-in, server connection, analytics service, advertising service, or cloud database to read, search, or bookmark content. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to the publisher or another company or organisation.

Bookmarks and reading-related preferences are kept on the device. The bundled reading text, notes, and fonts are also stored with the app. Removing the app may remove locally stored settings, subject to the device’s backup and operating-system behaviour.

The native app stores its reading content locally and includes an Internet permission. Its share caption includes a link to the public website. The website/PWA downloads app files and stores them in browser caches for offline use; hosting providers receive ordinary connection information when serving those requests. This is separate from the offline native reader. Website sharing sends selected pad text and a link; native sharing sends a pad image and caption through the chosen receiving app.

## Sharing and copying

At your request, the app can create a PNG image containing a full pad and its notes, or copy displayed text. When you choose a receiving app or person through the system share sheet, that recipient handles the shared material under its own terms and privacy practices. Pad Ratnakar does not operate the receiving app or service.

## Children

This app does not knowingly collect personal information from children because it does not collect personal information from users. The publisher must select the app’s actual target age groups in Play Console and update this policy if the app’s practices change.

## Changes

If the app’s data practices change, the publisher will update this policy and the Google Play Data safety declaration before releasing the changed version.

## Google Play Data safety draft

Subject to final binary, dependency, and network review, the intended declaration is:

- Does the app collect or share any required user data? **No.**
- Is data encrypted in transit? **Not applicable**, because the app does not collect or transmit user data.
- Does the app provide a data-deletion request mechanism? **Not applicable**, because the publisher does not hold user data.

Google requires every Play-published app, including one that collects no user data, to complete the Data safety form and provide a privacy-policy link. Closed, open, and production tracks require the form; an app active only on an internal test track is exempt. “Collect” includes data transmitted off-device by the app or its SDKs, so the publisher must review the final artifact rather than rely on this draft. See [Google Play’s Data safety guidance](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/10787469?hl=en-AE).
