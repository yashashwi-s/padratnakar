Pad Ratnakar is a simple offline reader for Hindi devotional literature.

FOUR COLLECTIONS
• Pad Ratnakar: 1,565 pads with source notes.
• Shodash Geet: an opening invocation, sixteen songs and a closing composition.
• Shri Bhaiji Jayanti Mahotsav ke Pad: 18 compositions.
• Mahabhav-Kallolini: 117 compositions organized by section.

READ COMFORTABLY
The reading layout preserves printed line endings and stanza structure. Pinch to enlarge the page, swipe between pads and save local bookmarks. Read black text on a white page with simple navigation.

FIND A PAD
Search in Hindi, type Hindi words using English letters, combine both scripts or enter a pad number. Filter by collection and use quotation marks to search an exact phrase. Results show the matching text.

SHARE AND COPY
Share a full image of a pad and its notes through an app you choose. Long-press the text to copy it.

AVAILABLE OFFLINE
Reading text, fonts and search are included. Reading, searching and local bookmarks do not require an internet connection or login.
